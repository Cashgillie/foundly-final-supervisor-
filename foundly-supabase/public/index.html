How Foundly Works

Three simple steps to reunite lost items with their owners, no account required.

1. Report an item

Tell us what you lost. Add details, location, and a photo to help identify it.

2. Browse & Search

Use search and filters to scan reports from your area. New items are posted daily.

3. Retrieve

Visit the Lost & Found Department for handover.

Found items should be submitted to the Lost & Found Department. The department will upload found items to the website to make identification and collection easier for the rightful owners.

Safety Tips

Leave out identifying features

If you found an item, don't describe every detail in your post. Leave out one identifying feature - a real owner will know it.

Never share sensitive info

Don't share your home address, financial info, or ID.

Report suspicious activity

If something feels off, don't proceed. Report suspicious listings to us immediately.

Privacy Policy

We take your privacy seriously. Here's the short version.

What we collect

When you report an item, we store the details you provide: item description, location, and contact email. We do not collect names, addresses, or payment information.

How we use your data

Your data is used solely to display listings and facilitate contact between finders and owners. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

Photos

Photos uploaded are stored to help identify items and are displayed publicly alongside the listing. You can request removal at any time by contacting us.

Your rights

You may request deletion of any listing or personal data at any time by emailing hello@foundly.app. We will process all requests within 48 hours.
